Next-Gen Smart Inventory Management

OptiStox keeps track of every item in your store. Receive stock, hand it out, check what is left and know when items will expire, all from your phone, tablet or computer.

Made for: Food and beverage factories, warehouses, hotels, restaurants and any business with a store.

Features

Everything you need, in simple and clear screens.

01

Receive stock

Record every delivery with date, lot number and expiry date.

02

Hand out stock

Issue items to production or staff and see the balance at once.

03

QR codes

Each item gets its own QR code. Scan with the built-in scanner.

04

Suppliers

Keep supplier details and see who supplied each lot.

05

Stock requests

Send stock requests by WhatsApp and email in one tap.

06

Expiry alerts

See items that are close to expiry before they become waste.

07

Three sign-in levels

User, Admin and Developer, each with the right access.

08

Cloud backup
